Why it is not a video

A recording cannot be asked a question.

An animation shows you one run of one experiment, chosen by whoever made it. Ask it what happens at forty-one degrees instead of forty and it has nothing to say. These are solved on every movement from the same few lines of mathematics the lesson is about — so the awkward value, the one a pupil actually asks about, is answered exactly like the tidy one.

It runs in the browser

No install, no plug-in, no account. Open the page and it is there, on the school's tablets as readily as on yours.

It runs offline

Once the page has loaded, the network is not needed again. A lesson does not stop because the school's connection did.

It is the same engine

These are the figures from inside the textbooks, lifted out and given their own page. Nothing was rebuilt to put them here.

Try one now
